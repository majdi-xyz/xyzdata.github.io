The problem

Group reporting shouldn't be a monthly spreadsheet fight.

01

Every site runs a different POS.

Ticketing from one vendor, F&B from another, retail from a third. Multiply by each park. Nothing exports the same way twice.

02

Numbers don't match between sites.

Is a season pass a ticket or a subscription? Does a combo meal count once or twice? Each site answers differently, so the group total is always in dispute.

03

Each new site means weeks of rework.

An acquisition or a new park lands, and someone rebuilds the reporting by hand. Again. The model never gets more robust, just bigger.

What we do

Three steps. Fixed scope. No open-ended consulting.

  1. Step 1 2 weeks · fixed price

    Data Diagnostic

    We map every source across your sites, find where the numbers break, and design the target model. You get a written report and a quoted scope for the build, whether you do it with us or not.

    • Inventory of POS, ticketing, F&B and retail sources per site
    • Reconciliation of current reports against raw data
    • Target model design and build estimate
  2. Step 2 6–8 weeks

    Unified Model build

    One model that turns every POS feed into daily revenue and visits per site, with the same product classification everywhere. Tested, documented, and owned by you.

    • Per-POS cleaning and normalisation
    • Shared product classification across sites
    • Daily revenue & visits per site, with automated tests
    • Runs on Snowflake, Azure, or BigQuery
  3. Step 3 monthly

    Ongoing data lead

    A senior data person on retainer. New sites get mapped in, new reports get built, and forecasting gets added when the base is solid.

    • Onboarding of new sites and new POS systems
    • New reports and dashboards as the business asks
    • Attendance and revenue forecasting
